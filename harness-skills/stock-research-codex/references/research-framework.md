# Stock Research Framework

## Source Priority

Prefer primary or direct sources first:

- Company investor relations pages, earnings releases, transcripts, presentations, and SEC/issuer filings.
- Exchange or regulator pages for listing, halt, corporate action, and rule information.
- Official macro sources such as central banks, statistics agencies, treasury departments, and regulators.
- Reputable market data and news sources for price action, consensus context, and recent events.

Use secondary commentary only after the primary facts are established. Do not treat social media, forums, or unsourced summaries as facts without corroboration.

## Research Chain

1. Define the exact question: trade timing, long-term investment, earnings preview, risk review, sector comparison, or post-mortem.
2. Identify the security precisely: ticker, exchange, share class, currency, and whether it is an ADR, ETF, option, or other instrument.
3. Collect fresh facts: latest price context, market cap, recent filings, earnings date, guidance, material news, and sector/macro facts.
4. Build the business read: revenue segments, growth drivers, margins, cash generation, leverage, dilution, and management guidance.
5. Build the thesis: what must be true for upside, what breaks the thesis, and what data would prove or disprove it.
6. Build scenarios: base, bull, bear. Keep assumptions visible and avoid false precision.
7. Produce a monitoring checklist: next events, metrics to watch, invalidation triggers, and review date.

## Evidence Standard

- Current market facts require browsing or a live data tool in the current turn.
- Filings and earnings claims should cite the exact document or company source when possible.
- If data sources conflict, state the conflict and prefer the more primary or more recent source.
- Never imply that old memory or stale notes are current market evidence.

## Shared Evidence And Arbitration

When the task benefits from role-separated analysis, follow
`evidence-agent-protocol.md`:

- Build one dated evidence set before assigning analysis roles.
- Limit the roles to fundamentals, market and technical, catalyst, risk, and
  valuation.
- Give every role the same evidence-set identifier and reject unknown evidence
  references.
- Degrade conclusions that cite no evidence; do not present them as facts.
- Admit only officially confirmed or multi-source-confirmed evidence into
  verified synthesis.
- Keep unverified evidence and unresolved contradictions visible, and reduce
  final confidence accordingly.
- Never emit broker actions, executable orders, trade-execution payloads, or
  automated-trading instructions.

OpenBB, yfinance, and AKShare may be used only as optional supplemental inputs
through the cloud data matrix. They do not replace mandatory local Tongdaxin
data for A-share K-line, limit-up, sector or board, capital-flow, fund-flow,
screening, or stock-selection work.
