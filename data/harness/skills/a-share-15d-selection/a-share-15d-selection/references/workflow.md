# Canonical execution workflow

1. The only external entry is `scripts/codex_entry.py`.
2. `run` always passes `skill_workflow_lock.py` and `stock_workflow_substantive_gate.py` through `stock-canonical-runtime-v1`.
3. The canonical adapter invokes `run_a_share_15d.py` as the primary business executor and gives it the receipt-owned `deliverables` directory. That executor scans current SH, SZ, and BJ TDX daily data once, applies the bound official holiday calendar, proves file-count closure with zero read errors, and writes the fixed JSON, CSV, and DOCX outputs from the locked template. A complete scan may truthfully return zero to five candidates; sparse results must disclose the actual count and must never be padded.
4. `audit_engine.py` independently rebuilds all scoring-pool cross sections and recomputes 26 factor stars, four axis totals, coverage, style profile, every hard gate, required-formula field contract, risk deduction, positive total, final score, deterministic rank, factor registry, forward-evaluation boundary, CSV/JSON field identity, and DOCX OOXML/content identity from persisted raw K-line, formula, market, evidence, concept, and state inputs, then writes `artifact_validation.json`. It never scans the market and never disables rendering.
5. Acceptance requires the current hash-bound runtime receipt, all four fixed deliverables, and a `CLEAN_PASS` artifact validation. Exit code, stdout claims, or file existence alone are insufficient. Microsoft Word open/page-count/PDF export and visual QA remain the required delivery verification after the canonical run.
